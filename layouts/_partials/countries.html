{{- /* The maps of a journey, the one with the most photos first. A photo belongs to the part of a country it lies in, or, on an island too small for the outlines, to the nearest part within a degree. A part less than 40 km across cannot be told apart on its own and draws in every part of its country within 100 km, which puts Norderney on the map of Germany. A smaller part without photos that reaches into the box of a map joins the smallest such map, so Sicily stays whole beside the mainland while Java stays off the map of Bali, and a photo off every coast, from a boat or a plane, joins the map whose box holds it most closely. A map whose photos all lie within a boundary from assets/geo/boundaries.json, a state of its country from assets/geo/states.json or a place their notes name, or close outside it, draws the smallest such boundary instead, and the country around it becomes the locator beside it. -}} {{- if partial "inside-part.html" (dict "lat" $cell.lat "long" $cell.long "part" $part) -}} {{- $match = dict "country" $c "part" $i -}} {{- break -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- if $claimed $i) -}} {{- $pb := index $country.properties.boxes $i -}} {{- /* a smaller part without a photo nearby joins the smallest box it reaches into */ -}} {{- $partSize := mul (sub (index $pb 2)
